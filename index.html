SWE-GPU-Bench: Can Language Models Solve Real-World GPU Software Engineering Tasks?

Feng Chen , Binbin Liu, Wenhan Han, Yin Zheng

Built an executable GPU software engineering benchmark derived from real-world CUDA and Triton repositories, covering bug fixing, feature implementation, and kernel performance optimization. Designed real-world task curation, executable environment transformation, automated functional testing, a performance regression pipeline, and isolated replay with dual Correctness/Performance gates to evaluate whether language models deliver patches that are both functionally sound and performant.

ICLR 2026 Poster · 3rd Author

Token Alignment Heads: Unveiling Attention's Role in LLM Multilingual Translation

Binbin Liu, Wenhan Han, Feng Chen , Yifan Zhang, Ping Guo, Haobin Lin, Bingni Zhang, Taifeng Wang, Yin Zheng

Uncovered sparse, cross-lingually consistent Token Alignment Heads (3.0%–7.9% of total attention heads across 1.7B–30B LLMs) via high-confidence word alignments and peak-attention Translation Scores on FLORES-101. Causal head ablation drops BLEU and chrF++ by over 17 and 25 points respectively whereas random pruning has minimal impact; our head-guided data filter TRater isolates the top 1.3% multilingual corpus whose 3× upsampling lifts FLORES chrF++ from 43.87 to 46.68.

Anchors First, Reason Later: A Layer-Wise View of Long-Context Processing

Feng Chen , Binbin Liu, Yin Zheng, Taifeng Wang

Investigated layer-wise positional sensitivity in long-context LLMs and formulated the Anchor-and-Reason hypothesis: shallow layers rely on fine-grained RoPE signals to localize tokens and form local semantic anchors, while deeper layers focus on global composition, retrieval, and reasoning with weaker positional dependence. Proposed LASP (Layer-Scaling for Position) to preserve strong positional resolution in shallow layers while progressively relaxing deeper constraints; verified via layer-wise RoPE masking, partial fine-tuning, and layer grafting, outperforming standard YaRN on HELMET (51.99 vs. 50.84) and six LOFT retrieval tasks (69 vs. 62/64).

arXiv 2024 Preprint · 4th Author

Factorized-Dreamer: Training A High-Quality Video Generator with Limited and Low-Quality Data

Tao Yang, Yangming Shi, Yunwen Huang, Feng Chen , Yin Zheng, Lei Zhang

Decomposed text-to-video synthesis into detailed text-to-image generation followed by image-and-motion conditioned video diffusion, integrating a T2I Adapter, Pixel-Aware Cross Attention, T5 motion encoding, optical-flow supervision via PredictNet, and noise schedule adjustment. Trained directly on ~19M low-quality and 300K high-quality public video pairs without an extra high-quality fine-tuning stage, achieving an EvalCrafter total score of 251 (surpassing compared open-source video generators, second only to commercial Gen-2 at 254).
